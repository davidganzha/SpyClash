### Review notes

```text
SpyClash is a multiplayer social-deduction game. Sign-in is required. Every app feature is available to every review account without a purchase.

SIGN-IN
Use the review email and password supplied in App Review Information. This account does not require OTP or access to an external identity provider.

CORE GAME
1. After sign-in, the Home screen offers Online and Local play.
2. Online: create a private room or join with a six-character code or QR invitation. iPhone and web-browser players share the same synchronized room. A full match requires at least three players.
3. Local: start a pass-and-play game on one iPhone; players read their role cards privately and pass the device to the next player.
4. The game supports Questions and Associations modes, voting, an early spy guess, rematches, custom word packs, friends by Spy ID, match history, and a leaderboard.

COMMUNITY SAFETY
1. Open Community from the command menu, select another operative, and use the profile actions to report or block that user.
2. Each profile-wall comment has its own Report action. Reports are private, stored in an admin-only moderation queue, and include a bounded snapshot for review.
3. Blocking removes existing comments and invitations between both accounts and prevents profile discovery, comments, invitations, and friend actions until the blocker explicitly unblocks.
4. Server-side filters reject objectionable profile comments and shared word-pack or room content; public avatars are restricted to the in-app catalog.

ACCOUNT DELETION
1. Open Profile, scroll to DANGER ZONE, tap DELETE ACCOUNT, and confirm the irreversible deletion.
2. The app deletes the account and associated user content, signs out immediately, and attempts to revoke its stored Sign in with Apple refresh credential.
3. If Apple revocation cannot be confirmed, including when no usable credential exists for a legacy account, the app completes account deletion and displays the manual Apple Account disconnect step.

ALL FEATURES AVAILABLE
AI theme and word generation, every profile avatar and style, complete match history, and advanced statistics are available immediately. The build has no pricing, purchase, restore, or paid-feature unlock surface.

PRIVACY LABEL CLARIFICATION
Contacts means only the in-service friend, request, and block graph; SpyClash does not access the device address book. Purchase History covers only limited retained records from retired billing programs. Other Diagnostic Data covers account-linked APNs and ActivityKit delivery state, bounded retry counts, and error codes used for notification delivery and troubleshooting.

The camera is optional. It is used to scan SpyClash room QR codes and, on supported devices, by ARKit Camera Assistance to stabilize local Nearby Interaction/Radar ranging while the app is open. Camera frames are processed on device and are not uploaded or stored.
```
